# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ANUBIS serves independent field technicians, one-person technical businesses, repair shops, microbusinesses, SMBs, supervisors, administrators, owners, and S&N platform operators. Field technicians use it in mobile, time-sensitive contexts while documenting a service on site. Owner, Admin, and Supervisor users use it on desktop or tablet to coordinate clients, services, team capacity, evidence, and reports.

## Product Purpose

ANUBIS is a commercial multi-tenant SaaS/PWA by S&N TI Soluciones Digitales for intelligent field service management. It exists to turn scattered service evidence into traceable, professional operation records: clients, services, assignments, work performed, findings, recommendations, photos, signatures/constancy, PDFs, and history. This first implementation is a professional foundation only: visual shells, mock data, design system, PWA baseline, documentation, and security-ready architecture without final business database migrations.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, and operationally premium. ANUBIS should feel like a trusted command surface for real work in the field: sober, fast, legible, and quietly distinctive. Egyptian inspiration is abstract and restrained through obsidian structure and bronze accents, never literal decoration.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS template, a game interface, neon cyberpunk, heavy gold luxury, pyramid or hieroglyph decoration, glassmorphism-heavy dashboards, fake testimonials, marketing overclaiming, ERP bloat, or dashboards that overwhelm technicians. Avoid UI that hides security boundaries behind frontend-only state.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence first: every screen should help users capture, review, or trust operational evidence.
2. Role-shaped workflows: technicians get fast mobile action; managers get dense but calm operational control.
3. Security is product behavior: tenant isolation, least privilege, privacy notices, and no frontend-only authorization assumptions.
4. Premium restraint: use visual polish through spacing, typography, contrast, state clarity, and disciplined motion.
5. Future-ready, scope-bound: prepare for Supabase, RLS, PWA, plans, and feature flags without implementing unapproved domain schema.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA from the foundation. Provide keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic HTML, labels, accessible error states, sufficient contrast, touch targets of at least 44px, reduced-motion support, responsive reflow, screen-reader names for icon controls, and mobile layouts that avoid hidden content behind fixed navigation.
